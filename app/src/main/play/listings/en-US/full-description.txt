Health Connect stores the data your watch, scale and fitness apps write — but there is no simple way to look at it. This app is that missing viewer.

WHAT IT DOES

• A dashboard of the figures you care about, one day at a time
• Browse every data type Health Connect supports, from steps and sleep to blood pressure and VO₂ max
• Individual records with exact timestamps, values, units and the app that wrote them
• Chart trends by day, week, four weeks or year
• Sleep and workouts shaded behind the day's charts, so a peak or a trough has an explanation
• Each workout with the heart rate recorded during it, and its route as a shape with an elevation profile, if you allow it
• Sleep stages — deep, light, REM and awake — for every night your watch records them
• A cycle overview: every cycle of a year side by side, aligned on day one, with bleeding, ovulation tests and temperature — only what was recorded, nothing predicted
• Trend arrows on the dashboard, comparing the last week with your 30-day average
• Streaks: how many days in a row you met your goal or were active, and the year's longest
• How each reading was taken: blood glucose fasting or after a meal, blood pressure seated or at the wrist, and with which device
• Arrange tiles by dragging, metric or imperial units, blood glucose in mmol/L or mg/dL
• Back up your dashboard and settings to a file and restore them on a new phone

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE

This app has no internet permission. Not "we promise not to upload anything" — Android itself prevents it from making any network connection at all. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

It is read-only: it can display your health data but cannot change or delete it. Nothing is written to disk unless you export it yourself, so uninstalling removes everything. Routes are read only if you allow it, and drawn without a map — a map would need the internet.

YOU CHOOSE WHAT TO SHARE

Permissions are requested one data type at a time, and nothing is selected by default. Allow only what you want to see, and change your mind whenever you like in Health Connect settings.

ACCURATE TOTALS

When several apps record the same thing — a phone and a watch both counting steps — their records overlap. This app uses Health Connect's own aggregation to remove duplicates, so daily totals are correct rather than double-counted, and it tells you when more than one app contributed.

Health Connect stores no link between a workout and the readings taken during it. Where this app shows a session's statistics, it says plainly that they are matched by time rather than pretending to a certainty the data does not support.

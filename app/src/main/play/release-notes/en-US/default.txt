0.4 — Readability, mostly. The navigation and status bar icons follow the app's own theme instead of the system's, so they are no longer white on white. Tiles flow into more columns in landscape and on tablets rather than growing enormous. Charts: the value axis lands on round numbers, steps and distance are drawn as bars, weight stays a connected line across missed weigh-ins, and a legend names every shape. A night is shown from the hour it began, not from midnight.

# Privacy Policy

**Health Connect View**

Last updated: 2026-08-28

## Summary

This app shows you the health data already stored on your device by Health Connect. It is a
viewer: it reads, it displays, and it does nothing else.

## Your data never leaves your device

The app has no internet permission. This is not a promise about our behaviour — Android
itself prevents the app from making any network connection. There is no server to send data
to, and no analytics, tracking or advertising code of any kind.

This is verifiable: the app's manifest is part of the public source, and the build fails if a
network permission is ever introduced, including through a dependency.

## What data is accessed

Only the Health Connect data types you explicitly allow. Permissions are requested one type at
a time and nothing is selected by default. The app reads this data solely to display it to
you on screen.

## Read-only

Only read permissions are requested. The app cannot create, change or delete your health
records.

## What is stored

No health data is stored. Records are held in memory only while you are viewing them and are
never written to disk. The app persists only display preferences, such as your selected time
range and theme.

Cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for this app, so nothing it holds is
copied off the device by the system.

## Sharing

Your health data is never shared with anyone, including the developer. No third-party SDK
receives it. The app contains no advertising or analytics libraries.

## Purchases

Optional paid features, if enabled, are handled entirely by Google Play. The app never
receives your payment details. Purchase verification happens through the Play Store app on
your device rather than over the network.

## Your control

You can withdraw access at any time in Health Connect settings, where you can also see
exactly which data types this app may read. Uninstalling the app removes everything it holds
— which is nothing beyond your display preferences.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published in the app and in this file in the public
repository, with the date above updated.

## Contact

Please raise questions or concerns through the issue tracker of the project's public GitHub
repository.

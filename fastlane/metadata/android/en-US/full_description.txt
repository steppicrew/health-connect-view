Health Connect View shows you the health data already stored on your phone by Health Connect. It is a viewer: it reads, it displays, and it does nothing else.

NO NETWORK ACCESS, BY CONSTRUCTION

The app does not request the internet permission. Not for analytics, not for crash reporting, not for anything. This is not a promise about how the data is handled — there is no code path that could send it anywhere, and the build fails if a dependency tries to add one.

Your health data is never written to disk by this app, never backed up, and never included in a device transfer. It lives in memory only while you are looking at it.

READ-ONLY

The app asks only for read permissions, and only for the data types you choose to grant. It cannot add, change or delete anything in Health Connect.

WHAT IT SHOWS

• A dashboard of the figures you care about, one day at a time
• Charts by day, week, four weeks or year
• Steps, heart rate, sleep, exercise, weight, calories, distance and more
• Sleep and workout sessions shaded behind the day's charts, so a peak or a trough has an explanation
• The raw records behind every number, including which app wrote each one

HONEST ABOUT THE DATA

Several apps often write the same metric, and they disagree. Totals come from Health Connect's own deduplication rather than from adding records up, and the app tells you when a figure is one app's rather than the combined total.

Health Connect stores no link between a workout and the readings taken during it, so where the app shows a session's statistics it says plainly that they are matched by time.

You need Health Connect installed, with data in it from the apps and devices you already use.
